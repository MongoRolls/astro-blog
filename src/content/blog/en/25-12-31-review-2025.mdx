2025 was unquestionably a year of enormous change. It brought not only the AI boom, but also major personal milestones such as graduation and starting work.

### Moving north to Beijing

I returned to Beijing's Xierqi area in March. Apart from taking a few days off to attend my graduation ceremony, I spent almost the rest of the year in Beijing.

During my internship in 2024, Hongshen and I joked that we might rent a place together if we got the chance. Unexpectedly, I really did receive a Beijing offer, while Hongshen also successfully became a full-time employee at Kuaishou. Since our offices were not far apart, we rented together.

Life in Beijing was actually fine. I saw snow for the first time, spent my first winter in the north, and even visited Harbin in temperatures below -30°C. During our time in Beijing, Jinyu, Martin, Zhirong, and the others also often got together for Haidilao.

### Interests and travel

My 2025 travel route: Tianjin, Beijing, Harbin, Guangzhou, and Shenzhen.

The most memorable trip was the **Northern Ring**. I deliberately chose weekdays after the National Day holiday to avoid the crowds, and had a wonderful time.

When you are young, you can travel and attend concerts. In the future, these become a **memory dividend**. You can go with your family and, as *Die with Zero* suggests, live a more valuable life instead of waiting until your parents are old: [notes on *Die with Zero*](https://ssshooter.com/die-with-zero/).

### Work

Work went smoothly overall. The luckiest part was the excellent team atmosphere and a very kind manager; nearly everything planned before I joined was achieved one by one.

### A few thoughts

1. **Stay clear-headed about major decisions**: leveraged actions such as buying a home can consume decades of future income and must be approached carefully. Short-video stories of people blindly opening restaurants—without research, with random franchises, and by gut feeling—often end in losses of millions; they are sobering.
2. **Delay gratification**: Zhang Yiming influenced me deeply. The simplest example is watching short videos only after finishing homework. This goes against human nature but requires deliberate practice. Start by not looking at your phone before leaving home in the morning and gradually change procrastinating habits.
3. **Deliberate practice**: in my experience, many things can be done very well if you prepare to retrieve them and deliberately train.
4. **Think about danger, retreat, and change**: borrowing a line from *The Ming Dynasty 1566*. In a favorable environment, remain alert to danger, keep an exit route for yourself, and look for new directions amid change.
