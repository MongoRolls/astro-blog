### Excalidraw collaborative diagram notes

- [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com/): hand-drawn diagrams.

### Metaso search

- [Text](https://metaso.cn/study)

An AI search tool that turns resources into course-like material in a slide format, with AI voice explanations and quizzes.

### Mermaid editor

[Mermaid editor](https://mermaid.live/)

Use Mermaid syntax to generate different diagrams (Gantt charts, flowcharts, ER diagrams, and more). It is useful for clarifying a project's ideas and asking AI to generate corresponding images; the same applies to diagrams for papers.

### DeepWiki

`https://deepwiki.com/{github-address}`

Generate a wiki from a project's documentation and publish it as a website with images, AI Q&A, and more.

### Zen

[Zen homepage](https://zen-browser.app/)

An open-source browser based on Firefox: [GitHub](https://github.com/zen-browser/desktop). It currently has more than 30k stars.  
The beta emphasizes privacy and calm. Its categorized sidebar tabs and overall design feel promising. I still mainly use Google, but it is fun to try.
